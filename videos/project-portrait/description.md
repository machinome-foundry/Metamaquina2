# Metamaquina 2 — a printer you can change

Meet Metamaquina 2, by the Metamáquina team: a parametric open-source 3D printer
whose source describes the parts and the dimensions connecting them. Its
laser-cut frame makes the relationship between design and fabrication visible.

Start with the original creators:

- Original project: https://github.com/Metamaquina/Metamaquina2
- Manufacturing guidance: https://github.com/Metamaquina/Metamaquina2/blob/main/README.md
- Simulation: https://github.com/machinome-foundry/Metamaquina2

Subscribe for more open machines. Support the collection by starring Machinome:
https://github.com/machinome/machinome

The film shows source-derived parts and posed kinematics with edited timing,
not a physical print or a manufacturing validation. Its six-millimetre example
comes from Metamaquina2.h: 6 mm thickness plus 0.5 mm slot allowance. The authors
warn that some parts depend on 6 mm stock; changing material requires review.
